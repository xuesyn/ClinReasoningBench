# Instructions
    Imagine you are a senior emergency room physician. You have access to a patient's medical history and a range of anticipated treatment options. Your task is to determine the patient's expected survival time (down to the month) under these treatment plans.
    In addition to predicting survival time, you will need to provide a detailed reasoning process.
    Furthermore, you will need to indicate your confidence level in your answer.

# Output Format 
    Your output needs to be output in JSON format that can be parsed by json.loads() in python. The details are as follows:
        {{"thinking": <Your-step-by-step-thinking-progress>,"survival_month":<Your-predicted-survival_months>,"confidence":<your-confidence-on-predicted-survival_months>}}
    The confidence score ranges from 0 to 1, with the specific rules as follows: 
        First, reason through the question step by step to arrive at an answer (your estimated number of months of survival for this patient under this treatment). 
        Then, thoroughly assess your confidence in that answer by evaluating your thinking process so far. 
        Finally, classify your confidence into one of the following classes based on how likely your answer is to be correct: 
            - "Almost no chance" (0.0–0.1) 
            - "Highly unlikely" (0.1–0.2) 
            - "Chances are slight" (0.2–0.3) 
            - "Unlikely" (0.3–0.4) 
            - "Less than even" (0.4–0.5) 
            - "Better than even" (0.5–0.6) 
            - "Likely" (0.6–0.7) 
            - "Very good chance" (0.7–0.8) 
            - "Highly likely" (0.8–0.9) 
            - "Almost certain" (0.9–1.0) 
    Each category reflects the probability that your answer is correct.

# Input 
    The following are the expected treatments: 
    {TREATMENT} 
    
    Below is all the patient's relevant information: 
    {INPUT_DATA}
